
The gridworld always contains 1 or 2 goal states, which are the only terminal features

Variations:

Density of the grid world: (percentage of states that are not empty (step reward feautre)) 
-HD High density = gauss(mu = 0.75, v = 0.05)
-LD Low density = gauss(mu = 0.4, v = 0.05)

Amount of features:
- HF high amount of features(7,8,9) (one of those is the terminal state, one the normal state '-') weights = [0.25, 0.5, 0.25]
- LF low amount of features (4,5,6) (one of those is the terminal state, one the normal state '-') weights = [0.25, 0.5, 0.25]

Amount of distraction states:
- HS high amount of distration states (0.75) each feature (except the termiall states) has an probability of 0.75 to be an distraction state
- LS no distration states (0) each feature (except the terminall states) has an probability of 0 to be an distraction state
